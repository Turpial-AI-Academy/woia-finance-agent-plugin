# WOIA Finance

Generic department orchestration v0.5.8 requiring Core >=0.5.7. Follow the five-stage [Finance skill](skills/woia-finance/SKILL.md) and [coordination contract](skills/woia-finance/references/finance-contract.md).

Finance coordinates obligations, accepted movements, eligible allocations, external formal settlements and separately approved payouts. The pure helper proposes governed Ledger/Payments operations and Customer Service delivery; it never dispatches, posts or accepts business facts itself. Qualified owners retain their ledgers, backends, policies and typed domain facts.
